Turn 2 · Redesign

Planner,
in print.

What the references share
  • Two tones only: ultramarine on warm paper, flipped from panel to panel. No greys, no shadows.
  • One oversized serif with tight tracking, ending in a full stop ("Authors."). A light serif for the lead-in text.
  • A quiet sans in small uppercase for nav and labels, with counts written as /128.
  • Hairline column rules form the grid. White panels are the only objects.
  • Lots of empty space, set asymmetrically. Some type is turned or overlaps.
Plan, mapped to the app
  • The month becomes the display title: "October." Week number and dates sit beside it in small caps.
  • Week / List / Today move to a top-right text nav, like Books / Authors. The sidebar keeps Subjects, Missed and tools.
  • Day columns are hairline rules. Day numbers are large light serif. Today is the one ink-filled block.
  • Cards become white panels with a serif title, a small-caps subject and a square checkbox.
  • Recurring bars become solid ultramarine bands, the reversed panel. Shading marks the days already done.
  • Subject colour shrinks to a 6px mark so the page stays two-tone. Missed is shown with ink and a count, not red.
  • Phone: same system. The tab bar becomes text nav, the bands get serif headings, and touch targets stay at 44px or more.
