Turn 4 · Phone, simplified

Fewer things,
bigger targets.

  • Day count is a 1 · 2 · 3 switch, 44px per segment. Arrows are gone; swipe the strip or the page to move.
  • One + button sits in the tab bar at bottom right, within thumb reach and never over content. The header + is removed.
  • Missed is a single tappable row instead of a badge plus a Details toggle.
  • 1 day: open rows, no boxes. Title first, subject and time on one line. Recurring shows a progress tick.
  • 2 and 3 days: soft grey cards with no borders, at least 64px tall. Subject names drop out at 3 days; the colour square carries it.
  • Today stays blue and bold everywhere. The shaded block marks which days are shown.

Turn 5 · Everything else

Sheets, not
screens.

  • Every action opens a bottom sheet over the current view. A sheet replaces another sheet; it never stacks.
  • One field style: borderless grey, 48px. One primary button: black pill, 50px, full width.
  • Dates are always the week strip from the header, so picking a day feels the same everywhere.
  • Destructive actions undo through a toast. Only deleting a subject, importing and resetting ask first.
  • The Planner tab holds what the desktop sidebar held: Missed, To-do, Subjects.
